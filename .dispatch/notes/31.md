`skill/templates/esp32/components/` with only its `core/`. It designs an LR4 low pass (sub) or high pass (mains) as
two cascaded Butterworth biquads, bilinear and pre-warped, for 40 to 200 Hz; runs them per sample (transposed DF II,
float, for the ESP32's FPU); and turns a sub's level (-60 to +12 dB, in mB like fwe_pd_limit) and delay (up to

-24.61 dB an octave out, flat passbands, the LP+HP sum flat to 0.05 dB from 20 Hz to 20 kHz, a step through each,
a 1 kHz sine through the sub, and the range refusals. esp32_test.py links `-lm` now (fw_test.py already did).
test_esp32_scaffold.py expects the new component and test. GOALS.md: #30 replaces "the dispatch/29 branch"; the
