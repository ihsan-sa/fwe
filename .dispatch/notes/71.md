them; any failed read returns -1 with plain 5 V, so `fwe_pd_limit_init` keeps the amp off. Host test with a fake bus
(a good read, and each of the three reads failing). CMakeLists comment, `fwe_stub/README.md` and GOALS.md updated.

goal "FWE writes and debugs the firmware, and plans bring-up with NPIE"). Both open goals are otherwise down to

has logic into host-tested `core/`, leaving the glue a thin I2C wrapper. No extra guard on a garbled read: the decode
already refuses a VSEL it doesn't know and a status with no PD bit, and a failed read now falls to amp off.
